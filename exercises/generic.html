Meet the Greek Gods

Discover four powerful gods from Ancient Greek mythology.

Ancient Greeks believed in many gods and goddesses. They had different powers and ruled different parts of the world. Let's discover four famous Greek gods!

1. Zeus ⚡

King of the Gods

Zeus was the ruler of Mount Olympus and the god of the sky and thunder. He carried a powerful thunderbolt and was considered the king of the gods. His symbols included the eagle and lightning bolt.

2. Poseidon 🌊

God of the Sea

Poseidon was the god of the sea, earthquakes and horses. He carried a three-pronged spear called a trident. He could create powerful storms and huge waves. His symbol was the trident.

3. Athena 🦉

Goddess of Wisdom

Athena was the goddess of wisdom, courage and strategic skill. She protected the city of Athens. Her famous symbol was the owl, which represents wisdom and knowledge.

4. Hades 🏛️

God of the Underworld

Hades was the ruler of the Underworld, the realm of the dead in Greek mythology. He was the brother of Zeus and Poseidon. He was often portrayed as serious and mysterious.
